### ▶ Cell 5 — How to EXTEND (🔁 cells)

- [ ] Done

Extend continues a clip from its **last frame**, so the face, room and light carry over.

1. Make sure the clip to continue (named in the cell) is **inserted** in your Vid.
2. In the AI video clip panel, click the **Extend** tab (next to Create / Edit / Animate).
3. Pick the clip to extend. Select it in the timeline first if Vids asks.
4. Paste the cell's **Paste this** box and set the length if offered.
5. Click **↑**, check it, then **Insert** and **mute** it.

**Extend rules**
- **Two extends at most in a row** (e.g. test clip → G4 → G5).
- Extending a long clip keeps only its **last 10 s** and adds the new part, so the new clip **starts with repeated footage**. Keep the original clip and trim the repeat in the edit. After that faces drift, so the next part is a fresh 🆕 Create.
- Never extend across a **change of place or a jump in time**. Those cells are 🆕 Create.
- If an extension goes wrong, redo **only that extension**, not the whole chain.
- If the Extend tab has no image slots, leave the @Image words in. They don't hurt.
